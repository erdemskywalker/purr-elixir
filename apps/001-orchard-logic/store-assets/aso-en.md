## 5. App Store Description

```text
Train your logic in a calm botanical orchard—no guessing, no timers forcing you, and no internet required.
Place one illustrated fruit in every row, every column, and every color region without letting any two fruits touch, even diagonally.
Start with gentle 4×4 tutorials and master flagship 10×10 grids crafted to have a single, mathematically verified solution.

SIMPLE RULES, DEEP DEDUCTION
• 1 per Row: Exactly one fruit belongs in each horizontal row.
• 1 per Column: Exactly one fruit belongs in each vertical column.
• 1 per Color Region: Ten distinct orchard zones—Strawberry, Grape, Lemon, Green Apple, Orange, Blueberry, Peach, Kiwi, Cherry, and Pineapple—each hold their own crisp vector fruit.
• No Touching: Fruits can never touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

DESIGNED FOR SPEED & COMFORT ON IPHONE
• Precision Tap & Double-Tap: Tap once to toggle a manual × mark; double-tap rapidly on an open square to place its fruit; tap a fruit to clear it.
• Drag-to-Mark ×: Glide your finger across empty squares to eliminate rows and regions effortlessly on 10×10 boards.
• Locked Auto-× Dots & Live Fruit Tray: Subtle dots highlight squares already blocked by an active fruit without cluttering your manual marks, while the color-changing Live Fruit Harvest Tray tracks every fruit left to place.
• Colorblind Fruit Markers: Toggle subtle corner fruit watermarks inside every zone for effortless accessibility.
• Calm Evolving Soundtrack & Juice: Unwind with an evolving acoustic orchard soundtrack, ascending harvest chimes, victory confetti, and shareable emoji result cards.
• Instant Rule Clash Feedback & Smart Hints: Explains every rule clash clearly, with unlimited undo, 2 free smart hints per board, and an optional rewarded video hint when you want a nudge.

CAMPAIGN + DAILY 10×10 HARVEST + ENDLESS MODE
• 30 Graded Pure-Logic Levels: Progress smoothly from 4×4 Tutorial, 5×5 Starter, 6×6 Warm-Up, and 8×8 Intermediate to 20 Master 10×10 orchards—zero blind guessing required.
• Daily Harvest (10×10): A 60-day rotating 10×10 challenge with daily streak tracking and personal best times.
• Endless Orchard: Generate fresh, mathematically unique 6×6, 8×8, and 10×10 boards on-device whenever you want more.
• Stats & Personalization: Track solved levels, daily streak & longest streak, endless completions, and per-size best times in Warm Ivory Light Mode (default) or Deep Espresso Dark Mode.

100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST
• Play anywhere—on flights, commutes, or offline retreats.
• No account, no login, and no cloud tracking: all puzzles, streaks, progress, and preferences stay privately on your device.
• Bilingual English & Turkish support built right into Settings.

Download Fruitopia today and unwind with crisp, pure deduction.
```
